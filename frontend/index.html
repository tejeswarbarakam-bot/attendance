Comprehensive System Features

Everything your institution needs to streamline attendance recording and reporting.

🎓

Student Attendance

Students can view overall percentage, subject-wise breakdowns, history records, and receive automatic shortage warnings.

👨‍🏫

Faculty Attendance Portal

Faculty can mark attendance for assigned classes, toggle bulk present/absent states, search students, and edit logs.

⚡

Real-Time Records

Instant synchronization with MongoDB Atlas database ensures zero delays and immediate availability of data.

📊

Attendance Analytics

Interactive dashboard analytics for admins to monitor department performance, monthly trends, and low-attendance alerts.

📄

Reports & Export

Generate student, subject, class, and date-wise reports with single-click export to CSV, PDF, or printable formats.

🔒

Secure & Compliant

Protected with JWT authentication, bcrypt password hashing, role-based authorization, and comprehensive audit logging.
